## background-and-culture

### [Pharisees](../articles/background-and-culture/pharisees.md) — Group

Read: Matthew 23:1-28; Mark 7:1-13; Acts 23:6-9; Philippians 3:5-6; Acts 5:34-39; John 3:1-2; Luke 13:31; Luke 7:36

Study questions:
- According to Acts 23:6-8, which beliefs did the Pharisees hold that the Sadducees rejected?
- In Matthew 23:2-3, what does Jesus tell the crowds to do with the Pharisees' teaching, and what does he warn them not to copy?
- Nicodemus (John 3:1-2) and Gamaliel (Acts 5:34-39) were both Pharisees. How do their stories complicate the picture of the Pharisees as simply Jesus's enemies?
- Jesus's sharpest warnings were aimed at devout, Bible-loving people. Where might Matthew 23:23-28 speak to our own church life today?
